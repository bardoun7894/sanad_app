## 2. Purpose, audience, value

Sanad is a mental-wellbeing app for Arabic, English and French speakers (Arabic-first, right-to-left). It helps adults who want emotional support and access to licensed therapists. It solves the difficulty of finding affordable, private, Arabic-language mental-health support. Value to users:

- Daily self-care tools: mood tracker, private journal, exercises, assessments (psychological tests), articles, podcasts and videos.
- An AI chat companion for supportive conversation and self-help guidance. It is not a therapist and does not diagnose.
- Chat, voice and video sessions with licensed human therapists, and booking of one-to-one sessions.
- A moderated community.
- Crisis detection that shows helplines when a user's text suggests risk.
- Gamification (streaks, challenges) to encourage regular use.

## 4. External services

- Firebase (Google): Authentication, Cloud Firestore (data), Cloud Storage (uploads), Cloud Messaging (push), Cloud Functions (server logic, including account deletion), Analytics.
- Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In: authentication.
- Google Gemini API (google_generative_ai): powers the AI chat companion.
- ZegoCloud: in-app voice and video calls with therapists.
- Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit): app subscriptions.
- Session booking payments (human therapy sessions): Freemius checkout (Visa / Mastercard), PayPal, and bank transfer confirmed over WhatsApp. Apple Pay is hidden on iOS in this build.
- Journal entries are stored locally on the device (encrypted Hive) and are not sent to any server.

## 5. Regional differences

The app does not gate any feature by country or region. Features, content and pricing logic are the same in all regions. The UI is Arabic-first, with English and French available. The crisis screen lists emergency helplines for Morocco, Saudi Arabia, UAE, the US text line and the international number 112, so users in any region see local numbers.

## 6. Regulated industry (mental-health therapy)

- Sanad is operated by HM5 Group.
- Therapists are licensed professionals. They are vetted by the clinic and must be approved by staff before their profile is activated and visible to patients.
- Licence documentation: [attach licence documents]
- Sanad is not an emergency service. The app states this and shows crisis hotlines. The crisis detection feature displays the "Emergency Helplines" screen when a message suggests the user is at risk, and alerts clinic staff.
- The AI companion is not a medical professional and does not diagnose.
- Community: posts and comments can be reported and are reviewed within 24 hours. Users can block other users. Users must agree to zero-tolerance community rules before posting. Staff moderate content from an admin dashboard.

## 7. In-App Purchase overview

Subscriptions are sold through Apple In-App Purchase only. Path: More, then Subscription Packages. Products (auto-renewing):

| Product ID | Plan | Length | What it unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| com.sanadtherapy.app.weekly | Weekly | 1 week | Text support, daily reminders, AI assistance (50 AI messages per month) |
| com.sanadtherapy.app.basic | Basic | 1 month | Weekly benefits plus assessments and monthly reports (200 AI messages per month) |
| com.sanadtherapy.app.premium | Premium | 1 month | Basic benefits plus a dedicated therapist and one monthly audio session (1000 AI messages per month) |
| com.sanadtherapy.app.premium_vip | Premium VIP | 1 month | Premium benefits plus three monthly audio sessions and a personalized care plan (unlimited AI messages) |

Prices are loaded live from the App Store via StoreKit and shown on the subscription screen for the user's storefront. Free accounts get 5 AI messages per day. The subscription screen shows the plan title, length, price, Restore Purchase, and links to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

One-to-one live therapy sessions with a licensed human therapist are a separate purchase. They are paid outside In-App Purchase, as permitted by Guideline 3.1.3(d) (person-to-person services), because the session is delivered live and one-to-one between a user and a therapist. On iOS the payment methods are: Visa / Mastercard (Freemius, shown only when configured), PayPal, and bank transfer confirmed over WhatsApp. Path: Therapists tab, choose a therapist, Book Session, then select a payment method.
